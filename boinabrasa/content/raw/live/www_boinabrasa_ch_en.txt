CATERING FOR YOUR PARTY AND EVENTS


For your events, we offer the possibility to pamper you and your guests at the venue with our Churrasco catering. For this purpose, we have a special smoke-free grill, so that the preparation of the tasty meat is possible even indoors without any problems.
Our professional barbecue party service also provides a selection of colorful delicious salads and side dishes, which will satisfy your guests all around.

DISCOVER CATERING

Restaurant Brazilian Rodizio

SOUTH AMERICAN PALATE PLEASURE

A steakhouse of a special kind: our professionally trained grill masters - the churrasqueiros - grill selected high-quality meat according to an age-old recipe on a rotating spit. The artful way of preparation guarantees an even cooking and a very tender taste. Enjoy up to 10 different varieties from the grill à discrétion, served directly at your table by our passadores. This traditional method of serving is called Rodizio, which literally means "the turning". At the buffet, you can help yourself to your heart's content and enjoy all our South American delicacies.
