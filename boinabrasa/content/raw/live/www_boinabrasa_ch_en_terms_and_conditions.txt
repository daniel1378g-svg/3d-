GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Terms and Conditions for Reservations and Groups

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Scope and Contracting Party

These Terms and Conditions apply to table reservations, group reservations and special events at Boinabrasa GmbH, Honeywell-Platz 1, 8157 Zurich Dielsdorf, Switzerland.

Any person making a reservation on behalf of a group, company, association or other persons confirms that they are authorised to do so and acts as the responsible contact and contracting party towards Boinabrasa.

2. Reservation and Conclusion of Contract

To make a reservation, you must have legal capacity and accept responsibility for all information provided under your name. All information must be accurate and complete.

A reservation becomes binding only when you have received confirmation from Boinabrasa by email or SMS. Requested changes become binding only after they have been confirmed by Boinabrasa.

Payment is generally made at the restaurant after the meal, unless a prepayment or deposit has been expressly agreed.

3. Group Reservations

A reservation for 7 or more persons is considered a group reservation, regardless of whether an individual quotation has been issued.

For group reservations, changes to the number of guests and other requests must be communicated as early as possible, and no later than 48 hours before the reserved time, using the personal reservation management page, by email or by telephone, unless a different deadline is stated in the quotation or reservation confirmation.

Change requests are reviewed by Boinabrasa and only become binding after written confirmation. An increase in the number of guests is subject to availability.

For group reservations of 7 to 9 persons, we accept a reduction of one person compared with the most recently confirmed number of guests. For group reservations of 10 or more persons, we accept a reduction of up to 10%. Unless otherwise agreed in the quotation, missing guests beyond the applicable tolerance may be charged as no-shows in accordance with Section 5.

The following additional conditions may be agreed for groups in particular:

a uniform menu or package selection, including a drinks package, for the group,
a minimum spend, prepayment or deposit,
one consolidated bill for the group.

Individual conditions stated in the quotation or reservation confirmation take precedence over these general provisions.

4. Cancellations and Changes

Reservations for up to 6 persons without an individual quotation: You may cancel directly through your personal reservation management page up to 4 hours before the reserved time. For cancellations at shorter notice, please send us a cancellation request or contact us directly.

Group reservations for 7 or more persons without a different agreement: Cancellations or significant reductions in the number of guests must be communicated to us no later than 48 hours before the reserved time.

Reservations with a quotation: The cancellation terms stated in the respective quotation take precedence.

Cancellations and changes may be communicated using the designated link or directly to Boinabrasa by email or telephone. The relevant time is when the notice is received by Boinabrasa. Please contact us as early as possible so that we can offer released seats to other guests.

5. No-Show and Late Cancellation

In the event of a no-show, late cancellation or missing guests beyond the agreed tolerance, Boinabrasa may charge compensation of CHF 30.– per affected person, unless otherwise stated in the quotation.

We always try to reallocate seats that become available at short notice. If the affected seats are successfully reallocated and Boinabrasa suffers no loss as a result, no no-show compensation will be charged. If only some seats are reallocated, compensation will be charged only for the seats that could not be reallocated.

To give us the opportunity to reallocate seats and, wherever possible, avoid costs for guests, cancellations and changes must be communicated as early as possible.

For special events or reservations involving a deposit, the conditions stated in the quotation or reservation confirmation apply. A deposit will be credited against the final bill or any compensation properly due.

6. Late Arrival

If you are running late, please notify us by telephone as early as possible. Without notice, Boinabrasa may release the reserved table to other guests 15 minutes after the confirmed reservation time. Different arrangements may apply to groups or special events.

7. Prices and Payment

All prices are stated in Swiss francs and include statutory VAT unless otherwise indicated. Prepayments, deposits, payment deadlines and any other payment terms are specified in the relevant quotation or reservation confirmation.

Children’s prices: Children aged up to and including 5 eat free of charge. This applies to no more than one child per full-paying adult. If the number of children in this age group exceeds the number of full-paying adults, a flat fee of CHF 15.– will be charged for each additional child. Children aged 6 to 12 inclusive pay 50% of the regular price of the selected meal or package. From the age of 13, the regular price applies. Drinks are not included unless they are expressly part of the offer. All children must be declared when making the reservation and count towards the total number of guests. Subsequent changes must be communicated to Boinabrasa as early as possible.

Where payment by invoice has been agreed, the complete billing address and all information required for invoicing must be provided to us in advance. The person responsible for the invoice must present an official identity document on site.

8. Data Protection

Personal data is used to process reservations, change requests and payments. Further information is available in our Privacy Policy.

9. Cancellation by Boinabrasa and Reservation of Rights

Boinabrasa may cancel reservations or restrict the reservation service for valid reasons, including force majeure, official orders, security risks, unforeseen operational disruptions, misuse or breaches of these Terms and Conditions.

Payments already made will be refunded unless the cancellation was caused by the conduct of the person making the reservation or their group, or unless services have already been provided or specifically ordered.

10. Final Provisions

Swiss law applies. Mandatory statutory rights and places of jurisdiction for consumers remain unaffected. If any provision of these Terms and Conditions is invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect.
