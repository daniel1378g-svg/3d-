DOLCEBRIGADEIRO

LET YOURSELF BE SEDUCED BY A NEW CHOCOLATE EXPERIENCE!

Dolcebrigadeiro is specialized for gourmet "Brigadeiro" ( Bree-gah-day-ro) and offers a new chocolate concept. All products come from our own recipe and are made only with natural ingredients and the finest chocolate.

Our handmade brigadeiros (truffle-fudge chocolate praline) and specialty candies are a delicious and creative way to celebrate and enjoy!

Ideal as original gift ideas or sweets for stores, weddings, sweetstables, birthdays and occasions of any kind.
