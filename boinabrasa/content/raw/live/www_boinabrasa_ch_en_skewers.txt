OUR SKEWERS FROM TRADITIONAL CUTS

Discover the variety of Brazilian churrasco: selected cuts of meat, grilled on skewers and served fresh at your table. From juicy picanha to slow-cooked costela – enjoy your favourites with our all-you-can-eat rodizio.

Fresh from the grill. Carved at your table. Brazilian gaucho style.

OUR SKEWER SPECIALITIES

Discover our different cuts of meat and what makes each one special.

YOUR PREFERENCES MATTER

Our skewers are served in a varied, random order. We do not keep a checklist of which specialities you have already been served.

Would you like a particular speciality or prefer your meat cooked differently? Please let our service team know during your visit so we can respond to your wishes straight away.

Picanha

Rump cap

The classic Brazilian churrasco cut. Its distinctive fat cap keeps the meat juicy and gives it a rich flavour.

Picanha com alho

Rump cap with garlic

Our picanha with aromatic garlic – savoury, juicy and full of flavour.

Maminha

Beef tri-tip

A delicate, flavourful cut of beef, grilled on a skewer and served in thin slices.

Costela de boi

Beef ribs

Slow-cooked beef with a rich flavour. The long cooking time makes it especially tender.

Alcatra com alho

Beef rump with garlic

Juicy beef rump with aromatic garlic, grilled on a skewer and freshly carved into thin slices at your table.

Presunto

Ham

Herzhafter Ham vom Spiess. Eine würzige Abwechslung zwischen den verschiedenen Fleischspezialitäten.

Costelinha de porco

Pork spare ribs

Juicy pork ribs with a rich grilled flavour – perfect for those who enjoy something hearty.

Linguiça

Brazilian sausage speciality

Savoury Brazilian-style sausage, freshly grilled and full of flavour.

Coxa de frango

Chicken thighs

Würzig grillierte Chicken thighs mit saftigem Fleisch und goldbrauner Haut.

Coração de frango

Chicken hearts

A traditional Brazilian speciality with a rich flavour and firm texture.

FOR OUR VEGETARIAN GUESTS

For our vegetarian guests, we serve grilled pineapple and grilling cheese. Please speak to our service team during your visit.

The selection of skewers may vary. Our team will be happy to tell you about the current selection and ingredients.
