== MAIN ==
Giardino - the distinctive Pizzeria in Leukerbad
Bella Italia in the heart of Bäderdorfs. Pizzeria Giardino, surrounded by an imposing mountain backdrop, is an experience for lovers of Italian cuisine. And a real insider’s tip for families with children. For the inside and outdoor playing areas alone.
Stylish elegance plays ingeniously with a cosy touch of wood to give Giardino its unique character with a lot of attention to detail.
The sun-drenched terrace and a clear view of the Gemmi and Torrent fits the Italian dream perfectly.
