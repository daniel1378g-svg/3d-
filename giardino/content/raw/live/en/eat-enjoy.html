Culinary delights

Fresh from the market, authentic, enjoyable. We ensure that we use the best quality not only when preparing our food, but also when buying it. With a great deal of passion and creativity, we conjure up both Italian and traditional delicacies. Our varied selection and the special children’s menu will capture the hearts of every gourmet, big and small. 

If you appreciate quality in a great atmosphere, Giardino in Leukerbad is the right place for you. Excellent Valais wines from the region and exquisite Italian tipples, which are also available by the glass, round off the complete experience of indulgence.

As a 100 percent WIR business we are also happy to accept WIR money.
