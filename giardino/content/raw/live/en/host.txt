== MAIN ==
It is nice being a guest - but feeling at home is even better!
Since 2012, we, Pascal and Anja Loretan, have been continuing the treasured and long-standing tradition of hospitality in Giardino in Leukerbad into its second generation. We will also be happy to pass on the unique pleasure of pampering our guests to both our sons, Janis and Niklas. The two little rascals have already taken their first tentative steps in customer service.
“If our guests are happy and satisfied, then we are too” - we live by this philosophy every single day with our young, dynamic team. Two of our service fairies and kitchen imps have been looking after the guests at Giardino for many years.
Our hosts’ hearts are in the right place. Would you let us show you what we can do?
We are looking forward to your visit!
Pascal and Anja Loretan & Team
