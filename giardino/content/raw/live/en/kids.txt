== MAIN ==
A restaurant without children???s laughter is just a place to eat ...
But Giardino in Leukerbad is a child’s paradise. You are the most important people at our restaurant. You will find lots of yummy things to eat on our extra child-friendly menu.
But there is a lot more to enjoy than that. So that you don’t have to sit at the table for hours, we have set up a playroom for you to run around in. And in summer you can also romp about in the big outside playground.
Shhhh! We will let you in on another secret: From summer 2016, there have an enormous pirate ship for you to conquer.
