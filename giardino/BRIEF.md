## 1. Non-negotiables and priority order

When requirements conflict, this order wins: **(1) truthfulness** > **(2) content completeness** (every page, photo, menu line) > **(3) accessibility and performance** > **(4) repository conventions and hand-off** > **(5) art direction** > **(6) motion and 3D**. If time runs out, cut in reverse order: 3D first, then set-piece motion, then galleries' extras. Never cut menus, hours, reservation honesty, legal pages or a language.

Non-negotiables:
1. No invented facts: no dishes, prices, allergens, dietary claims, awards, ratings, reviews, founding stories, chef names, wood-fired ovens, amenities, opening hours, floor plans or availability. Copy may only restate what the sources say.
2. Research is read-only: GET requests only. Never submit the live contact form, never e-mail or phone the restaurant, never create accounts (felizzio, TripAdvisor, Facebook, Google).
3. The reservation flow never shows "confirmed" (bestätigt, confirmée, confermata) in any language. A build-time grep over `dist/` fails on those words.
4. Every string exists in all four languages; a missing key fails the build. German is never rendered inside an EN, FR or IT page.
5. Primary content (menus, hours, phone, reservation form, legal) is server-rendered HTML that works with JavaScript disabled, without WebGL, and with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. No third-party requests in proposal mode: self-hosted fonts and libraries, no analytics, no cookies, no CDN scripts, no auto-loaded embeds (maps, Matterport, YouTube get a two-click facade).

## 6. Art direction: Italian garden meets Alpine elegance

- **Palette as tokens, derived from the logo.** `--surface` warm ivory; `--surface-deep` charcoal for evening sections; `--ink` charcoal text; `--brand` the logo's olive family (`#29381b`, `#a9ad81`) for decorative and brand uses; `--accent` terracotta, used sparingly for the reservation action only; `--brass` a muted metallic for hairlines and small ornaments. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (3:1 for display type ≥ 24 px); no gradients except a single warm vignette on photography; no glassmorphism.
- **Type.** Two Google Fonts families, self-hosted WOFF2, latin + latin-ext, ≤ 4 files / ≤ 250 KB, preloaded, `font-display: swap` with metric-compatible fallbacks so swap causes no reflow. An expressive editorial serif for display (shortlist: Instrument Serif, Fraunces, Cormorant Garamond) and a quiet humanist sans for UI and menus (shortlist: Inter Tight, Manrope, IBM Plex Sans). Fluid display scale from 2.5 rem at 360 px to 7 rem at 1440 px with `text-wrap: balance`, tested against the longest strings in each locale ("Manger et savourer", "Kulinarische Gaumenfreuden") so nothing overflows at 360 px.
- **Composition.** 12-column grid, one spacing scale, a named set of asymmetric compositions (5/12 text beside 7/12 image, offset stacked photo pairs, a full-bleed photograph every two viewports at most), generous negative space, no repeating card grids. Textures: one subtle paper grain on ivory surfaces and one botanical line motif derived from the logo's olive branch; no stock textures.
- **Photography.** Only the restaurant's own photographs. Keep originals untouched in `media/originals/`; generate AVIF + WebP at 480/960/1440/2400 widths, never wider than the source (full-bleed only for the ≥ 4000 px 2025 set; the 1600 px 2012 and 2016 sets live in editorial frames ≤ 60 vw); one shared colour grade; crops chosen per image with `object-position`, no automatic centre crops of people or dishes; no AI upscaling or generative fill. Write factual alt text in four languages (what is visibly in the picture, no dish names). Every one of the 48 photographs appears somewhere, galleries included; `assets.json` records each file, its source URL, pixel size, where it is used, and the rights note.
- **Video.** Show the 120-second video only as a user-initiated player with the poster frame, muted autoplay forbidden; offer a trimmed, re-encoded ≤ 8 MB 1080p version only if quality holds, otherwise poster + play.

## 8. Motion system and scroll choreography

- **One vocabulary.** Entrances 350–600 ms ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`), micro-interactions 120–180 ms, set-pieces ≤ 1200 ms, staggers 40–70 ms; animate only `transform`, `opacity` and `clip-path`; no scroll hijacking (no Lenis in combination with ScrollTrigger pinning); every animated element is visible at full opacity in the pre-rendered HTML so content never waits for JavaScript.
- **Set-pieces, at most three on the home page and one elsewhere:** the hero resolve; one scroll-linked food-and-atmosphere story with masked image reveals and layered parallax (depth ≤ 12 % of travel) that is pinned for ≤ 150 vh and can always be scrolled through; the language switch (§9). Everything else uses calm reveals: headline and paragraph entrances, animated link underlines, button press feedback, image tilt ≤ 2° on hover.
- **Header.** Shrinks from 88 to 64 px over the first 80 px of scroll with `transform` only, gains the deep surface after the hero, keeps the reservation action visible at all times (header on desktop, fixed bottom bar on mobile, 44 px targets).
- **Page transitions.** Multi-page static build with the View Transitions API where supported: 250 ms crossfade, back/forward restores scroll, focus moves to the new `h1`; no SPA router, no transition longer than 300 ms.
- **Never animate:** prices, hours, the closure notice, the reservation summary, form fields, validation messages, the phone number, focus rings, or text metrics (no split-text layout animation).
- **Reduced motion and mobile.** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → no intro, static reveals, parallax, tilt and pinning off, 3D → poster, 150 ms crossfades only. On touch devices: no parallax layers beyond one, no pinning, galleries scroll natively with visible previous/next buttons and dot indicators.

On selection: (1) 0–140 ms the outgoing interface strings clip out horizontally; (2) 0–400 ms the logo travels right along the lane by `min(240 px, free lane width)` and back on a single ease-in-out curve, and `html lang`, title, meta and route (`history.pushState` to the equivalent path, carrying query and hash) switch at the midpoint; (3) 240–600 ms a horizontal `clip-path` reveal follows the logo's leading edge and introduces the new strings; (4) the logo settles pixel-exactly into its resting box. Reduced motion: 150 ms crossfade. Zero CLS during the switch, measured.

## 13. Engineering quality and budgets

- First-route JavaScript ≤ 180 KB gzipped excluding the 3D chunk; fonts ≤ 250 KB; above-the-fold images ≤ 300 KB total; explicit `width`/`height` on every image; `loading="lazy"` below the fold; no layout shift from fonts, header, hero swap or language switch.
- Targets, measured headlessly with Lighthouse (Playwright Chromium, mobile emulation, 4× CPU throttle, "Slow 4G") on `/de`, `/de/essen-geniessen`, `/de/reservation`: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, CLS ≤ 0.1, TBT ≤ 200 ms as the lab proxy for INP; report numbers with the exact conditions and note that headless Chromium has no GPU, so WebGL paths are measured as the poster fallback.
- Accessibility WCAG 2.2 AA: axe-core 0 critical/serious on every route; full keyboard operation of selector, menus, galleries, calendar and forms; visible focus ≥ 3:1; `aria-live` for form results; skip link; lightbox and panels trap focus correctly and restore it.
- SEO (launch mode only): localised titles and descriptions, canonical, hreflang, sitemap, Restaurant JSON-LD with only verified fields (name, address, phone, URL, kitchen hours as `openingHoursSpecification`, `servesCuisine: Italian`, no ratings or awards). Proposal mode emits `noindex` and a disallow-all `robots.txt`.
- Artifact preview: `npm run build:artifact` → `artifact/index.html` plus files, relative paths, Google Fonts as the only external stylesheet, the permanent band, poster instead of 3D; publish it and record the URL in HANDOFF.

## 15. If blocked, and never

If blocked: OCR unavailable → visual transcription with `verification: visual`; a line unreadable → unresolved, shown as "Preis siehe Karte"; the Leukerbad Therme, hoteljob or Matterport targets unreachable → keep the link with a "status unverified" note in HANDOFF; a translation uncertain → `needs-review`; no form endpoint → proposal transport as §10; a fact missing → omit it and ask in Open questions. Never: fabricate, submit real forms, contact the restaurant, rehost the PDFs or video in `dist/`, show "confirmed", add third-party scripts, animate prices, hide the phone number, or report a check you did not run.

The result should feel like entering Giardino before arriving: warm light on wood, the olive branch, the Gemmi beyond the terrace, and a table that is easy to ask for. Give it extraordinary visual ambition, and make every fact on it true.
